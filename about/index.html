Where is the moisture coming from?

A ceiling stain, unexplained moisture, or pressure loss can have more than one cause, so document the symptoms before opening a wall. For a leak concern in Petrolia, photograph stains and nearby plumbing, note when they appear, and record any change in the water meter when fixtures are off.
